# FONTLOG for Mallige

Mallige is an open-source font for Tulu lipi, designed by Prahlad Prasad Tantry and
released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see `OFL.txt`).

A TrueType font with glyphs in the Unicode Tulu-Tigalari block as well as Latin-encoded glyphs.
